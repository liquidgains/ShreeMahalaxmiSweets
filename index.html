Our Story

A Sweet Legacy Born in Solapur

Welcome to Shree Mahalaxmi Sweets — Solapur's cherished destination for authentic traditional Indian mithai. Founded two decades ago with the blessings of Goddess Mahalaxmi and a passion for purity, we have grown into a beloved institution serving thousands of families across Maharashtra.

Every sweet we make is crafted fresh daily by our master halwais using 100% pure cow's ghee, real saffron, and the finest dry fruits — no artificial colours, no preservatives, no shortcuts. Just the timeless taste of tradition.
